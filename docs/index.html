Bangladesh RJSC & International Incorporation

Corporate governance, tax registration, BASIS membership, and global banking.

Compliance

Phase A: Bangladesh Pvt. Ltd. (RJSC)

  • Name Clearance: Apply at roc.gov.bd for EMON INDUSTRY PVT. LTD.
  • MOA & AOA: Cover Software, Cybersecurity, IoT Hardware, Print & Health-Tech.
  • Incorporation: File Form IX, Form XII & digital signatures for Certificate.
  • Tax & Trade: Obtain Company e-TIN (NBR), Trade License & 13-digit BIN (VAT).

Phase B: Banking, BASIS & Global Scale

  • Corporate Bank & ERQ: Open BDT & USD Export Retention Quota account.
  • BASIS Membership: Register with BASIS for IT/software tax exemptions.
  • Global Billing: Enable Wise Business / Payoneer / SWIFT for global clients.
  • International Holding: Expand via Delaware C-Corp or Singapore Pte. Ltd.
